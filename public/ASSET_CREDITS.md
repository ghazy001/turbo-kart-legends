## Generated runtime derivatives

`assets/optimized/` contains local runtime derivatives of the nine supported supplied models listed above. Generated by `scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` using Three.js's MIT-licensed Meshopt simplifier; triangle topology/vertex storage are reduced while embedded author/license/source metadata, materials, images and skeleton bindings are retained. Originals remain in their supplied folders. `docs/assets/optimization-report.json` identifies every derivative and its source SHA-256. Runtime static fitting and driving-only prop removal do not add authored animation clips. Existing local-use permissions and source-credit caveats still apply.
